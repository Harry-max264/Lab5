# Diffusion factor assessment and management judgment

| Factor | Assessment for quotation assistance | Basis | Evidence that would change it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relative advantage | Potential time saving, unproven after review | Inference; no measured local benefit | Manual vs assisted total task effort and errors |
| Compatibility | Decisive constraint: confidential, bilingual and auditable work | Case assumptions; S4 ecosystem roadmap | Authorized workflow with traceable summaries and no manual re-entry burden |
| Complexity | Easy interaction may conceal difficult verification | Analytical inference | Correction burden for quantities, currencies and clauses |
| Trialability | Offline historical review can bound exposure | Proposed design; S3 adjacent pilot signal | Data permission unavailable or redaction removes usefulness |
| Observability | Activity is visible; reliable gains are not established | S3 named pilot and S4 roadmap | Independently reviewed benefit and sustained use |

General technology: broad observed use, no universal Rogers label. Application: reported adjacent sourcing pilot and productization plans. Industrial peer population: unknown prevalence. Fast follower: learn and collect references now; early adopter: prioritize controlled local evidence; conservative: wait for stronger references before live participation. All preserve human award authority.

Strongest signal: named sourcing pilot. Main inhibitor: compatible, reliable review within the organization. Adoption Agent caution: procurement experimentation is not proof of mainstream diffusion, ROI or safe autonomy. Sources and dates: evidence_register.md.
